02 — ProblemGlint · Y-Prize 2026–27 · Phase 1

Organoids are replacing animal tests.
But every one is a black box.

The FDA's Roadmap to Reducing Animal Testing explicitly names organoids and organ-chips. FDA 2025

01 · Destroy
The standard viability assay lyses the sample.
CellTiter-Glo 3D gives one reading — then the organoid is gone.
02 · Surface-only
Imaging sees only the surface.
Tissue scatters light. Whole-well readouts average it all — they can't tell core from rim.
03 · Scarce
Patient-derived tumor organoids are scarce.
Destroying them for a single time point is the costliest habit in the workflow.
Electrodes don't fix it
Missing: a quantitative readout that doesn't destroy, in every well, over time.
Sources: FDA, Roadmap to Reducing Animal Testing in Preclinical Safety Studies (Apr 2025)02 / 05
